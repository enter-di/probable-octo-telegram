Asian Range Breakout

3-Phase Execution

Phase 1 — Overnight (00:00–08:00 London)
Bot records the overnight consolidation high and low
Phase 2 — London Open Watch (08:00–10:00)
Bot monitors for a decisive close outside the range
Phase 3 — Execute
Entry on confirmed breakout candle, SL at opposite side of Asian range

Parameters

InstrumentGBPJPY only
Valid range size0.5× – 4.0× ATR
Take Profit1.5:1 Risk-Reward
Trend filterH1 EMA 50/200

Why This Works

Asian session creates a defined price range as liquidity is low. At London open, institutional order flow breaks the range with strong momentum — a predictable daily pattern in forex markets.

The bot captures this move with a superior 1.5:1 risk-reward ratio — higher than the momentum strategy — because breakout moves tend to run further after a clean range hold.

Once per day, per symbol — no repeat entries
Range too tight or too wide → no trade (volatility filter)
