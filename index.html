Infrastructure
MetaTrader 5

MT5 is the trading platform that connects to FTMO's broker. The bot talks to it via the MetaTrader5 Python library — a local API running on the same Windows VPS, so there's no network hop between the bot and the terminal.

get_candles(symbol, timeframe, count) get_all_open_positions() place_order(symbol, direction, volume, sl, tp) close_position(ticket) get_closed_deal_profit(ticket) get_symbol_info(symbol) # spread, lot limits

Algo trading must be enabled manually in MT5: Tools → Options → Expert Advisors → Allow Algo Trading. Without this, every order placement silently fails.

Main Loop
New M15 candle?

The main loop runs every 15 seconds. On each tick it checks whether a new 15-minute candle just closed — comparing the last completed candle's timestamp against what it saw previously.

ref_candle_time = ref_data.index[-2] # COMPLETED candle is_new_candle = ref_candle_time != last_seen if not is_new_candle: manage_open_trades(new_candle=False) # cleanup only sleep(15) continue # New candle — full scan manage_open_trades(new_candle=True) scan_all_symbols_for_signals()

iloc[-1] is the forming candle — its data changes every tick. iloc[-2] is the last closed bar — stable, reliable. Using [-1] for signals was a bug in v1 that caused false entries on incomplete candles.

Trade Management
Manage open trades

Called every loop. The new_candle flag controls what it does:

manage_open_trades(new_candle=False) → Detect trades closed by broker (TP/SL hit) → Send WIN/LOSS notification + real P&L → Update consecutive loss counter manage_open_trades(new_candle=True) → Everything above, PLUS: → Increment bars_open for each open trade → bars_open >= 32 → force close (8h time exit)

The 8-minute bug (fixed in v3): bars_open was incrementing every 15-second loop. 32 × 15s = 8 minutes. Both live trades on day 1 were killed at exactly 8 minutes. Fixed by gating the increment behind new_candle=True so it only counts actual M15 bar closes.

Fake WIN bug (fixed in v2): When a trade closed via TP/SL, the P&L was accidentally reading the account balance. WIN notifications showed +$100,000.71. Fixed with get_closed_deal_profit(ticket) querying MT5 deal history.

Entry Checklist · 01
DD limits & position cap

Daily 3% limit: If today's equity is already 3% below where it started, no new entries. FTMO allows 5% — the 2% buffer means even if trades in progress lose, you won't breach the challenge limit.

Overall 7% DD: If equity is 7% below the initial challenge balance, no new entries. FTMO's hard limit is 10% — 3% of headroom remains.

Max 3 simultaneous positions: Never more than 3 trades open at once. Caps the maximum one-event loss to 1.5% (3 × 0.5%) even if USD shocks all positions simultaneously.

Entry Checklist · 02
No open trade on symbol

Maximum 1 open trade per symbol at any time. If EURUSD already has a live position, no new EURUSD signals are taken until it closes.

This prevents averaging down — adding to losing positions hoping for a reversal. Every signal is evaluated independently. The bot never doubles up on a symbol regardless of the current position's P&L.

Entry Checklist · 03
12-bar cooldown (3 hours)

After entering any trade, that symbol is locked for 12 M15 bars = 3 hours. The cooldown applies regardless of outcome — win, loss, or time exit.

This prevents revenge trading — immediately re-entering after a loss. Three hours is enough time for the original market condition to fully resolve, so any new signal is based on fresh price action rather than residual noise from the previous trade.

Entry Checklist · 04
Session filter — 08:30 to 19:30

Only trades during the London open through New York close. The Asian session (before 08:30) has thin volume on EUR/GBP pairs — momentum candles there often represent a single market maker moving a thin book, not real institutional flow.

The London-NY overlap (13:30–17:00) is peak global liquidity and where the backtest shows the strongest win rates. After 19:30 the NY session thins and edge degrades.

Entry Checklist · 05
Friday close window

All positions close at Friday 20:00 server time. No new entries after this. No positions held over the weekend.

Forex markets are closed Friday evening through Sunday night. During those 48 hours, geopolitical events, central bank announcements, or surprise economic data can move prices significantly. When markets reopen, price can gap past your stop loss — you get filled at a far worse price than intended. On a $100K challenge account, one bad weekend gap can wipe a week of gains instantly.

Entry Checklist · 06

Strategy
Asian Range Breakout

GBPJPY only. Measures the overnight consolidation range and trades the breakout when London opens.

asian_high = max(high, 00:00–08:00) asian_low = min(low, 00:00–08:00) range = asian_high - asian_low # London open (08:00–10:00): close > asian_high + bullish candle → BUY close < asian_low + bearish candle → SELL SL = opposite end of range + 0.3 ATR TP = 1.5:1 RR

Why GBPJPY? GBP moves sharply at London open. JPY consolidates tightly during the Asian session. The combination creates consistently clean overnight ranges that break decisively when London participants arrive. The large pip movements on GBPJPY make the strategy profitable after spread costs.

Execution
Position sizing

Every trade risks exactly 0.5% of current balance. Lot size is derived from the stop loss distance so dollar risk is always constant.

risk_amount = balance × 0.005 sl_distance = ATR × 1.3 pips_at_risk = sl_distance / pip_size lots = risk_amount / (pips_at_risk × pip_value_per_lot) # EURUSD example ($100K, ATR = 8 pips): # risk = $500, SL = 10.4 pips # lots = 500 / (10.4 × 10) = 4.81 lots

Assessment
Challenge pass probability

Current estimate: 68–70%

Working in our favour: All critical bugs fixed across 4 versions. Backtest shows 57.5% WR over 7 consecutive profitable months. EURUSD PF of 1.80 is a genuine edge. Spread filter prevents bad fills during news. Risk management is genuinely FTMO-compliant with real buffers.

Still concerns: Live WR will likely drop to 52–55% due to slippage. At 1:1 RR that's a thin margin. EURUSD + AUDUSD + EURJPY are all USD-correlated — one Fed surprise can hit all three simultaneously for 1.5% in one candle.

Three things to push to 75%+:

1. RR 1.0 → 1.3 — backtest first, needs 2 weeks of demo data. At 1.3:1 you only need 43.5% WR to break even.

2. Correlation guard — limit to 1 USD-correlated position at a time. Eliminates the 3× simultaneous loss scenario.

3. News filter — block entries 30 min before NFP/FOMC/ECB. The spread filter catches during-news spikes but not the pre-news momentum candle.
